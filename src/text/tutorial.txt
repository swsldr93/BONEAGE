# Tutorial pages: each appears the first time its moment comes up, until the player has seen it or turns the tutorial off.
# [attack] etc = the key or button for that action. {PREY()} = kangaroos/wallabies, {PREY1()} = kangaroo/wallaby,
# {PRED[predK]} = the line for the predator met (pred.* below). Anything else in {...} is game code: leave it as it is.
# A blank line = new paragraph.

== light
This fire is burning. Face it and thrust your spear into the flames with [attack] to light the spear.

A burning spear lights your way, holds predators off, and can carry fire to a cold camp. It burns out after a while, so relight it whenever you pass a fire.

== near
You are near a small gathering of {PREY()}.

Press [crouch] to sneak, and move slowly towards them.{((pl.spearLit>0&&pl.armed==='spear')?'<br><br>Your burning spear will alert them from much further away. Put it out first with '+b('douse')+'.':'')}

== aim
You are within striking range.

Hold [aim] to raise your spear and aim. The {PREY1()} should follow the point of your spear tip.

When you are ready to throw, keep holding [aim] and press [throw].

== lost
Your spear is stuck in the animal, and it is getting away.

The spear will work loose and drop after a few seconds. Follow it and walk over the spear to pick it up.

Until then you have your knife. [swap] switches between spear and knife.

== eat
Small kills: press [eat] over the kill to take it up. Big kills (kangaroo, diprotodon, marsupial lion, megalania): hold [eat] to cut meat from it, and carry that instead.

Carry the meat to a burning fire to cook it.

Walk over your spear to pick it up again.

== lay
Press [eat] to lay the meat down by the fire.

Then thrust your spear into it with [attack] to take a piece on the point.

== cook
Face the fire and thrust your spear into it with [attack]. You hold the meat in the flames while it cooks.

When it is done, hold [eat] to eat. Eating fills the hunger bar along the bottom of the screen and mends wounds.

Take the next piece from the meat by the fire the same way.

== scav
A predator is eating the kill you left behind.

Drive it off and you can take back what is left. Or leave it to them.

== camp
A cold fire. Thrust your burning spear into it with [attack] to light it.

Every fire you light in an area makes it safer: by a burning fire you grow hungry more slowly, and predators hang back for a while, but in the end they will come in after you: light your spear to hold them off. With two lit, your wounds heal beside them.

== smoke
That thin plume of smoke rises from a cold fire.

Follow the smoke to find the other camps, and carry a flame to light them.

== pred
{PRED[predK]}

If you can, get away: climb up onto rocks or squeeze into a narrow slot in the cliffs. Predators will not follow you there.

A burning fire only holds them back for a while. Light your spear in it and hold the flame out at them: keep them off long enough and they give up and leave.

== stand
You are holding them off with fire. While your spear burns they will not close on you.

Step towards them and they give ground. Stand still and they slowly edge back in on you.

Do not press them too hard: rush at them and they may take it as a challenge and turn on you. Keep them at bay long enough and they give up and leave.

== wound
You are wounded. The red at the edges of your sight shows how badly.

Eating a kill heals a wound. Once two fires burn in an area, resting beside one slowly heals you too.

== hunger
You are getting hungry. The bar along the bottom of the screen shrinks as you do, from green to red. When it is gone, you starve.

Hunt and eat to fill it. Hunger comes on more slowly while you rest by a burning fire.

== night
Night is coming. Predators grow bolder in the dark, and prey is harder to see.

Stay close to a burning fire, or carry a flame on your spear.

== tired
You are out of breath. Running with [run] tires you quickly.

Walk for a while and your strength returns.

== book
Something has been left here. Press [eat] to pick it up and read it.

[fwd] turns the page forward and [back] turns it back. Press [eat] again to put it down, or carry it with you.

== exit
This path leads out of the area, to the next part of the country.

Keep walking to cross over. Fires you have lit stay lit, and you can come back.

== pred.thy
Thylacines have found you. They hunt in packs, circling you and taking turns to attack.

== pred.lion
A thylacoleo has found you. It hunts alone, and attacks by leaping at you from a distance.

== pred.mega
A megalania has found you. It is huge and terribly strong, and you cannot outrun it.
