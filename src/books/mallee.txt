Elmes,
== t
I entreat you to grant safe passage for food up through the Downs to the Eromanga coast.
== t
Nearly seven centuries ago my ancestors led the charge through your borders in the Northern Tablelands and down to the coast.
== t
Today we don’t even have a tenth of those numbers, and those we have are buckling at the knees from starvation.
== t
I know you’re not any better. I’ve seen the abjection of your peons, and the tattered banners on your border.
== t
Neither of us can afford to lose more men.
== t
Our terms:
== h3
Article I: Cargo
== t
Four caravans of grain require transit.
== h3
Article II: Route
== t
The convoy will take a direct path through the Downs to the Eromanga coast, terminating in Inner Sahul.
== h3
Article III: Escort
== t
A minimal force will accompany the grain to deter raids.
== h3
Article IV: Guarantee
== t
Grant this passage, and we’ll withdraw all other forces from near the borders.
== t
For the first time in generations, our people will know peace.
== t
As a gesture of good faith, I’ve sent one of my captains from the Eromangan fleet to personally deliver this message.
== t
I trust he will be treated as well as you are able, and ask that you kindly spare an escort for his return through the North Bassian Range.
== t
In faith,
== s
Cpt. Fascian
Eromanga
